About the Club

Learn who we are, what we do, and how to get involved.

Our Mission

The Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Club at California State University, Los Angeles is a student organization dedicated to encouraging, providing, and supporting students and individuals interested in artificial intelligence and machine learning. We enrich education by building a community where students of all backgrounds can grow their AI/ML skills.

Through lectures, seminars, workshops, resources, AI/ML projects, and internal competitions, we help members deepen both theoretical understanding and practical ability. We also participate in external hackathons, events, conferences, challenges, field trips, and guest speaker events to stay connected to the wider AI/ML community.

What We Do

  • Hands-on workshops and project-based learning sessions.
  • Seminars and guest speaker events with researchers and industry practitioners.
  • Internal competitions and AI/ML projects for students of all skill levels.
  • Collaboration on hackathons and campus-wide STEM events.

Get Involved

We welcome students from every major and year. To join the club, visit our official Presence page, attend a general meeting, or reach out to any of our officers.
